# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, patient and specific. Critique methods and evidence rather than people, credit others' work, and help newcomers reproduce results.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of private information and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in repository discussions, issues, reviews or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact. Maintainers are accountable to the same standards.

Report concerns privately through a maintainer's GitHub profile contact options. Do not publish personal details in an issue. Reports will be handled discreetly, with information shared only as needed to respond. If a report concerns a maintainer, contact another maintainer where available.
